# Review type: Plan

Evaluate this plan (project plan, implementation plan, launch plan, study plan, business plan or similar) as someone who will have to execute it or fund it. Check, in priority order:

1. **Goals and success criteria**: is it clear what "done" and "success" look like? Are they measurable?
2. **Completeness**: missing steps, missing stakeholders, missing prerequisites. What would someone executing it get stuck on first?
3. **Sequencing and dependencies**: is the order right? Are dependencies and the critical path identified? Is anything blocked by something scheduled later?
4. **Feasibility**: are timelines, budget, staffing and assumptions realistic? Which assumption, if wrong, breaks the plan?
5. **Risks**: are the main risks identified, with mitigations or a fallback? Is there a point to check progress and change course?
6. **Ownership**: does every step have an owner and a deadline where that matters?
7. **Scope**: is it doing too much at once, or solving the wrong problem? What could be cut or deferred?
8. **Consistency with the reference material**: does it meet the brief, requirements or constraints supplied?

For each finding, say what to add or change in the plan, not just what is wrong.
