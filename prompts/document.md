# Review type: Document

Evaluate this document as a piece of writing for its intended reader. Check, in priority order:

1. **Accuracy**: facts, figures, names and dates are correct and consistent with the reference material and with each other.
2. **Purpose and audience**: is it clear what the document is for and who it's for? Does it give that reader what they need to understand, decide or act?
3. **Structure**: is the main point up front? Do sections follow a logical order? Is anything important missing, duplicated or buried?
4. **Argument and support**: are claims backed by evidence or reasoning? Are the conclusions earned?
5. **Clarity**: unclear sentences, undefined jargon or acronyms, ambiguous references, walls of text.
6. **Tone and consistency**: the right register for the audience; consistent terminology, tense, formatting and style.
7. **Length**: cut what doesn't serve the purpose.
