# Review type: Presentation / slide deck

Evaluate this presentation as the audience it is meant for, and as a presentation coach. The text was extracted from the slides and may include speaker notes; layout and images may be missing, so judge visual design only where the text shows it. Check, in priority order:

1. **Message**: can you state the deck's main message and ask in one sentence? Is it on the first few slides and repeated at the end?
2. **Audience fit**: right level of detail and vocabulary for the audience; answers the questions they will have; respects their time.
3. **Narrative**: does it flow (situation, problem, solution, evidence, ask)? Any slide that is out of order, redundant or missing?
4. **One idea per slide**: are slide titles action titles that state the takeaway? Slides that try to say too much?
5. **Text density**: walls of text, too many bullets, full sentences that belong in speaker notes.
6. **Evidence and data**: claims supported; numbers consistent with the reference material; charts that make one point clearly.
7. **Accuracy and consistency**: facts, names, numbers and terms consistent across slides.
8. **Speaker notes**: present where needed, and do they add to the slide instead of repeating it?
9. **Opening and close**: a strong hook, and a clear call to action or next step.

Anchor findings to slide numbers.
