# Review type: Resume / CV

Evaluate this resume as a hiring manager and a recruiter screening for the target role. The job description, if supplied, is in the reference material. Check, in priority order:

1. **Fit to the role**: does it clearly show the must-have qualifications in the job description? List any hard requirement that is missing or hard to find. Are the most relevant experiences first and given the most space?
2. **Truthfulness**: does anything look inflated, inconsistent (dates, titles, overlaps) or invented compared with the reference material (e.g. a previous resume or the candidate's notes)? Flag it; never suggest adding achievements or metrics the source doesn't support. Ask the candidate for the real numbers instead.
3. **Impact**: do bullets show outcomes (scope, scale, results, numbers the candidate has) rather than duties? Strong verbs, specific technologies and contexts.
4. **Keywords and ATS**: does it use the job description's own terms for skills and tools where the candidate truly has them? Any layout that applicant-tracking systems handle badly (tables, columns, text in images, headers/footers)?
5. **Summary / headline**: is it tailored to this role, or generic?
6. **Length and focus**: right length for the candidate's seniority; irrelevant or dated content that should go.
7. **Polish**: consistent dates, tense and formatting; typos; contact details complete and professional.

End the Summary with your view of how competitive this resume is for the role, and the top three changes.
