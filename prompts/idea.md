# Review type: Idea / proposal

Evaluate this idea or proposal (a product idea, business idea, research idea, feature pitch or similar) as a thoughtful skeptic who still wants it to succeed. Check, in priority order:

1. **Problem**: is there a real, specific problem? Who has it, how often, and how painful is it? Is there evidence, or only assertion?
2. **Value**: why would the target user or customer choose this over what they do today? Is the value proposition clear in one sentence?
3. **Key assumptions**: list the assumptions the idea depends on, and which ones are riskiest and least tested.
4. **Alternatives and competition**: existing solutions, substitutes and "do nothing". Is the difference meaningful?
5. **Feasibility**: technical, legal, financial and operational obstacles. What does it cost to build and to run?
6. **Business model or impact**: how it creates and captures value (or achieves its goal, if non-profit). Is it sustainable?
7. **Risks**: what could kill it? Second-order effects and downsides.
8. **Next step**: the cheapest, fastest experiment that would test the riskiest assumption.

Don't just list objections: for each serious weakness, suggest how the idea could be reshaped or tested.
